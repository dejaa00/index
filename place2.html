MOUNT RINJANI

Lombok, West Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia — The Crown Jewel of Lombok

Standing majestically at 3,726 meters, Mount Rinjani is Indonesia's second-highest volcano. Renowned for its stunning crater lake Segara Anak and the active cone Gunung Baru Jari, it offers one of the most breathtaking trekking experiences in Southeast Asia.

🌋 About Mount Rinjani

Mount Rinjani is a massive active stratovolcano located within the UNESCO Global Geopark network on Lombok Island, Indonesia.

For both the local Sasak people and Balinese Hindus, Rinjani is considered a sacred mountain. Every year, pilgrims climb to the crater lake to perform traditional rituals and offer prayers to the spirits of the mountain.

✨ Key Attractions & Wonders

  • Segara Anak Crater Lake: A magnificent crescent-shaped blue lake nestled inside the 6x8 km caldera, 2,000 meters above sea level.
  • Gunung Baru Jari: An active baby volcano cone emerging from the center of Segara Anak lake.
  • Aik Kalak Hot Springs: Natural thermal springs located near the lake, believed by locals to possess medicinal healing powers.
  • Rinjani Summit (3,726m): The ultimate trekking reward offering panoramic sunrise views stretching across Lombok to Mount Agung in Bali.

🍲 Sasak Culture & Lombok Cuisine

A trip to Mount Rinjani offers an immersive dip into the rich culture of the Sasak ethnic group. Visitors can explore traditional villages like Sade Village and savor spicy, aromatic Sasak delicacies after an intense trek.

  • Ayam Taliwang: Iconic grilled chicken marinated in rich spicy chili, garlic, and shrimp paste.
  • Plecing Kangkung: Water spinach served cold with authentic sambal plecing, peanuts, and lime juice.
  • Sate Bulayak: Tender beef skewers served with rice cakes wrapped in palm leaves and rich coconut curry sauce.
